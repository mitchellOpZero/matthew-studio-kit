# matthew (default)

Built from what Matthew asked for: his crimson captions, 1.5x bigger, keywords only; snap punch-ins; the real app logos when he names them; nothing jammed at the top; and "the basics" (no music, no stacked sound effects, no clutter).

## Every video in this style
- Captions: white, one line, 2 words, at chest level. Each keyword turns crimson as he says it. About one keyword per caption, and only words that carry the meaning.
- Zooms: snap between wide and tight about every 2.5 s. Hard cuts, no easing.
- Logos: when he names YouTube or Instagram (or another app we have a logo for) near the start, show its logo tile once.
- Sound: his voice only, at -14 LUFS.
- Never: graphics that just repeat his last few words; anything under the top or bottom app UI; music.
