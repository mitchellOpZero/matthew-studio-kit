# matthew-long

Matthew's look for long-form YouTube (16:9). Same rules as `matthew`, sized for a landscape frame.

## Every video in this style
- Captions: white, one line of up to 4 words in the lower third. Each keyword turns crimson as he says it. About one keyword per caption.
- Zooms: snap between wide and tight about every 4 s. Hard cuts, no easing.
- Logos: when he names YouTube or Instagram near the start, show the logo tile once.
- Screen recordings and app demos: b-roll with `"layout": "pip"` (his face in the corner), so he never disappears for long.
- Pictures (screenshots, photos): on a card beside his head, never over his face.
- Sound: his voice only, at -14 LUFS.
- Never: graphics that just repeat his last few words; music; stacked sound effects.
